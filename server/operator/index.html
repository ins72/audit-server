Payments

Enter your Stripe secret key and webhook signing secret. They are stored on the server, never sent back to browsers (only a masked hint is shown), and take effect at once — no restart. In Stripe, set the webhook endpoint to PUBLIC_URL/billing/webhook. Once set, “Pay on web” in the app opens Stripe Checkout and a completed payment unlocks the account automatically.
Leave a field blank to keep the saved value. Clearing falls back to the environment (STRIPE_SECRET_KEY / STRIPE_WEBHOOK_SECRET), if set.
